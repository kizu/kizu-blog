Originally, I wanted to share some other stuff, but then realized that maybe I am too tired for that. Like, I decided on what I want to share, started gathering the links and thinking how I would describe those…

Then thought that I want this month to maybe end already? I started finishing those posts later and later in the day, with the last few being published after midnight by my time, so, essentially, on the wrong day.

It is my blog, and I do what I want, so this time for the _Share Saturday_ I will just share that I am tired.

Also! Unlike other posts, each of which I shared on Mastodon, I think I won’t share this one. Also, I will add a feature to my blog where this post won’t go to its RSS.

There will be a gap there, but not on the website itself. I still wrote something. If you visit it — you will read it.

Hello, reader, I guess? Nice of you to read this random post without me linking to it.

In the future, I might even create a feature where a post will be hidden _everywhere_. Although, for that, I have some ideas over a way to do this kind of semi-private website, where while the content will stay, the links will rot, as I could republish everything under uniquely hashed urls or something. That needs more thought.

And with that, I will stop writing this post.

I need to make me some more tea, the current cup is cold.
