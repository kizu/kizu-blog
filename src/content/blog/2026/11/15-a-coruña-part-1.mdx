Two years ago, in June, I participated in a CSS Working Group’s face-to-face meetings in [A Coruña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Coru%C3%B1a). After they ended, I stayed for another day, and walked along the coast for a few hours.

I took a few photos, but only published the first few — on my way towards the coast. I still need to go through all other photos and publish them!

To motivate myself to do so, I will start posting what I already published here, and hope that this will motivate me finish this part of my backlog eventually.

---

After the conference, I stayed in a hotel with a pretty bad location, but the way from it to the coast was pretty pleasant, once I passed some industrial areas.

The day started clear, there was a lot of greenery, and a slightly hilly path alongside a road.

![A bus station](pixelfed:868210158927501335)

At some point, I chose to go through a small village of sorts. There was a cat. I did not meet anyone else there.

![](pixelfed:868215901265800129)

I went directly through the village, and it ended with a path along its wall, with some nice wooden fences with ferns and hills behind them.

![Exiting the village](pixelfed:868219372568358469)

From this point, you could spot the ocean behind the hills, with the serpentine roads going along them. On the side closer to me, I saw some goats.

![And they spotted me](pixelfed:868600340018395829)

Look at this one. There was like a very long chain, but I did not spot where it ended. They seemed to have quite a lot of area available to eat!

![](pixelfed:868602127525477371)

Further along, the path narrowed, going through the ferns, with occasional pine trees standing lonely, and the ocean appearing and disappearing from behind the hills and the ferns.

![](pixelfed:868603779147464017)

Almost near the coast, there was an interesting bench. I was not yet tired enough, and was eager to continue walking.

![Nice concrete bench](pixelfed:868830116688475225)

This part of my journey ended right near the coast, with more serpentine roads, with occasional cards and cyclists going up the road, and tall grass all around.

![](pixelfed:868932986524441679)

Next time (and most of all the remaining photos) will be the ocean itself, with some coastal views.
